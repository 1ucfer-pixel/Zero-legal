# Zero Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 3, 2026**

Zero ("the Bot") is a Discord bot operated by **Valhalla** ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the Bot stores, why, and how you can have it removed. By using the Bot or adding it to a server, you agree to this policy.

Contact: **the Zero support server (https://discord.gg/yuXPgzHDMu)**

## 1. What we collect

The Bot only collects data needed to run its features. This is what it stores in its database.

**Server data**
- Server, channel and role IDs, and settings chosen by server admins (log channels, welcome and goodbye texts, automod, ticket and panel setup, and similar).

**Moderation data**
- Moderation cases, warns, strikes, jail and restriction records, timed actions, and staff notes. These include user IDs, reasons, and who acted.
- Blacklists and anti-raid or anti-nuke whitelists (user and server IDs).

**Member activity and progression**
- XP, levels, coins, inventory, streaks, achievements, quests, pets, game stats, profiles and bios, marriages, and similar per-member data.
- Counts of messages per member, channel and hour, used for stats and leaderboards. The messages themselves are not stored for this.
- Nickname history (old and new nicknames).
- Command usage records.

**Content you deliberately submit**
- Suggestions, reminders, polls and votes, giveaway entries, tickets and ticket transcripts, reports, birthdays, AFK messages, custom commands and autoreplies, music playlists, and notes you ask the AI to remember.
- **Confessions:** if the confessions feature is enabled, your user ID is stored alongside the confession so that staff can act on abuse. It is not shown publicly.

**Optional third-party accounts**
- If you link Last.fm, we store your Last.fm username.

## 2. What is kept only briefly in memory

- Recently deleted or edited messages (for `.snipe` and server logs). These are held in memory and are not written to the database.
- If AI is enabled for a server, recent chat used for AI replies and the daily story. This is held in memory only.

We do **not** keep a general log of chat messages in the database, and the Bot does not read direct messages sent to other people.

## 3. Message content

The Bot requests Discord's Message Content and Server Members intents. It reads messages in servers to run commands, automod, scam detection, levelling, and the optional AI features. It does not store message text beyond what is described above.

## 4. AI features and Google

AI is off by default and is only active in servers where it has been enabled. When it is active, the text of a request (and, for the daily story, a sample of that day's chat) is sent to **Google's Gemini API** to generate the response. Google processes that text under its own terms and privacy policy. Servers without AI enabled never send anything to Google.

Members can opt out of some features, such as the daily story (`.storyoptout`) and roasts (`.roastoptout`).

## 5. How we use data

We use data only to operate, secure, and improve the Bot: running commands, moderation, leaderboards, tickets, and fixing bugs. We do **not** sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties, except the AI processing described above and where required by law.

## 6. Storage, backups and security

Data is stored in a database on the server where the Bot is hosted. Automatic backups of the database are kept for a limited period (by default the last 7 backups). We take reasonable steps to protect the data, but no system is completely secure.

## 7. Retention and deletion

- **Server owners** can run `.deletedata` to erase everything stored for their server.
- If the Bot is removed from a server, that server's data is deleted automatically after **30 days** unless the Bot is re-added.
- **Members** can ask a server admin to remove their records, or contact us at the address above and we will delete the data tied to your user ID. Deleted data may remain in backups until they rotate out.

## 8. Children

Discord requires users to be at least 13 (or the minimum age in their country). The Bot is not intended for anyone under that age, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child's data is stored, contact us and we will remove it.

## 9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version. Continued use of the Bot after a change means you accept it.
